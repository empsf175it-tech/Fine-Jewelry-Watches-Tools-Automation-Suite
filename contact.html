04 / FAQ

Questions before the next step.

What is ChronoForge for?

It is a concept for a dedicated web application supporting fine jewelry and watch service operations, tools, automation and administrative visibility.

Can the workflow be customized?

Yes. The product direction is designed around configurable stages, roles, permissions, checklists and approval rules.

Are login and signup part of this version?

The public marketing experience has intentionally removed standalone login and signup pages from this refined version. Authentication can be introduced as part of the actual application build.

Is the contact form connected to a backend?

No. It is a front-end demo interaction and should be connected to your preferred email or CRM service for production.
